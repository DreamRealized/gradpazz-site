Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify my .edu email?
Go to Profile > Verify .edu Email. Enter your university email address and we'll send you a 6-digit verification code. Enter the code to get your verified badge.
How do I create a listing?
Tap the "+" tab at the bottom of the app. Fill in the title, description, price, category, and upload photos. You can also mark items as free or negotiable.
How do I delete my listing?
Open your listing, tap the trash icon at the bottom, and confirm. Deleted listings are removed from all feeds and search results.
How does chat work?
Tap "Chat" on any listing to start a conversation with the seller. Messages are delivered in real-time. You can also share images and listing cards in chat.
How do I block or report a user?
Open a conversation with the user, tap the menu icon in the top right, and select "Block" or "Report." You can also report listings by long-pressing on any post in the feed.
Can I use the app in Chinese?
Yes! Gradpazz supports both English and Simplified Chinese. The app automatically detects your device language. You can also switch manually in Profile > Settings.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your profile, listings, messages, and all associated data. This action cannot be undone. You can also request deletion by emailing support@gradpazz.site.
Is my data safe?
We take privacy seriously. All data is encrypted in transit, authentication tokens are stored in iOS Secure Storage (Keychain), and we enforce row-level security on all database tables. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Report an Issue

If you encounter a bug or have a feature request, you can:

  • Email us at support@gradpazz.site
  • Use the in-app report feature (long-press any post or message)

When reporting a bug, please include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue.

Safety Tips

  • Always meet in public, well-lit campus locations for exchanges
  • Verify the seller's .edu badge before transacting
  • Never share personal financial information in chat
  • Report suspicious listings or users immediately
  • All transactions are peer-to-peer and off-platform
